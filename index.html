System Architecture

Engineered for absolute accountability.

Modern autonomous agents can produce erratic commands. DeskFlow introduces mathematical rigor, execution boundaries, and posttrade verification into the Binance Agent OS tool chain.

SAFEGUARD ONE

Immutable Mandate Boundary

DeskFlow loads governing rules directly from configuration. Regardless of prompt complexity, orders requesting margin, futures, or leverage multipliers are rejected before accessing any order routing endpoint. Parent order capital is strictly capped at ten dollars notional value.

Permitted Venues Convert · Spot
Prohibited Venues Futures · Margin · Perps
Capital Cap $10.00 Notional Max
SAFEGUARD TWO

Scoped Subaccount

All writes occur strictly within the dedicated subaccount. Main account balances, external transfer capabilities, and withdrawal methods are denied by protocol design.

SAFEGUARD THREE

Dual Venue Arbitrage

Real time evaluation compares Spot order book depth against Convert pricing. Guarantees true market liquidity selection with zero synthetic or fabricated quotes.

SAFEGUARD FOUR

Implementation Shortfall Audit

Following fill confirmation, DeskFlow calculates execution shortfall in basis points against original decision mid pricing, while keeping exchange fees isolated to ensure clean slippage measurement.

Shortfall BPS = ((Execution Price minus Decision Mid) / Decision Mid) * 10000

Operator Inquiries

Frequently addressed questions.

Understanding DeskFlow boundaries, subaccount security, and execution mechanics.

Why is human authorization mandatory?
Autonomous language models can generate unintended trades through prompt drift or hallucination. Requiring explicit syntax confirmation guarantees that capital is never committed without human intent.
How are exchange fees handled?
Trading fees are isolated in a dedicated column on the blotter. This prevents slippage calculations from conflating market impact with fixed fee structures.
Can DeskFlow access master account balances?
No. All operations are strictly bound to the AGENTIC_SUB subaccount. Master account funds and external withdrawal mechanisms remain completely unexposed.
What happens if market data feeds drop?
If public book ticker endpoints fail to return reliable depth, DeskFlow halts the execution pipeline immediately with a data rejection error instead of inventing artificial quotes.
